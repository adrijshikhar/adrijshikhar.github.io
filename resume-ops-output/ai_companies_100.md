# AI Companies — Backend Roles Tracker

**100 companies · 4 YOE · Remote-preferred · Snapshot: May 2026**

A curated list for backend engineers (~4 years experience) targeting AI-focused companies. Includes AI-native startups, AI infrastructure, AI dev tools, vertical AI SaaS, data infra adding AI, big SaaS with AI initiatives, and India AI-native companies.

---

## How to use this list

**Fit score (1–5)** reflects match strength based on: skill alignment with backend / distributed systems / data infra background, India-remote viability, and YOE level fit.

- **5** — Strong direct fit. Apply first.
- **4** — Good fit. Worth tracking and applying.
- **3** — Plausible fit. Selective; apply if specific role appears.
- **2** — Weak fit. Mostly on-site US/EU or very selective.
- **1** — Long shot.

**Buckets:**

| Code | Bucket | What it means |
|------|--------|---------------|
| A | Frontier AI lab | Companies whose primary product *is* the AI model |
| B | AI infrastructure | Inference, training, GPU cloud, ML tooling |
| C | AI dev tools | Coding agents, IDEs, agent frameworks |
| D | AI vertical SaaS | AI built for a specific industry |
| E | Data infra + AI | Streaming, CDC, ELT, vector DBs (your direct Hevo overlap) |
| F | Big SaaS + AI | Established SaaS adding AI as a major investment |
| G | India AI-native | AI companies founded/HQ'd in India |

---

## Strategy notes

1. Apply first to **Bucket E (Data infra + AI)** — your CDC/Debezium/Hevo work is direct competitive signal there.
2. Bucket B (AI infra) is your second-best technical match: same distributed-systems primitives, applied to GPU/inference.
3. For frontier labs (Bucket A), expect bar to be very high. Mistral is the most realistic EU option.
4. Remote-first companies that hire India globally: Hugging Face, Vercel, Sourcegraph, Supabase, dbt Labs, Pydantic, Weights & Biases, ElevenLabs, Replicate.
5. India-remote with strong eng: Confluent BLR, Databricks BLR (newly expanding), MongoDB, Glean BLR, Atlassian, Stripe, Notion Hyderabad.
6. India on-site standouts: Sarvam, Atomicwork, Composio (smaller, faster IC growth).
7. Pitch angle: *"Senior backend at Hevo (ETL/CDC/Debezium), shipped real-time CDC framework, schema service, 15x scaling. Now want to apply distributed systems to AI infra."*
8. "Remote" on listings often means "remote within country" for tax reasons. Always confirm India-eligibility before investing time.
